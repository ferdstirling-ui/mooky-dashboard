Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026.

Mooky dashboard is a private tool used only by its owner, on their own computer.

What it reads

With the owner's permission, given on Google's sign-in page, it reads the owner's own YouTube channel statistics through Google's official YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API. It uses read-only access. It cannot upload, change or delete anything.

Where the data goes

The numbers are saved only on the owner's own computer. They are not sent to any server, not shared with anyone, not sold, and not used for advertising.

Google user data

Mooky dashboard's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can remove the tool's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and delete the saved numbers from their computer.
